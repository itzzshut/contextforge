# Data Retention and Privacy

## Where data is stored

Workspace data is stored in data centers in the United States by default. Business plan customers can request EU-only data residency at signup or by contacting support; migrating an existing workspace's region takes about 2 weeks.

## Deleted note retention

Deleted notes go to a workspace Trash folder and are permanently erased 30 days after deletion, unless a member restores them from Trash first.

## GDPR data requests

EU users can request a full export or deletion of their personal data under GDPR by emailing privacy@nimbusnotes.example. We respond to data subject access requests within 30 days as required by law.

## Encryption

All data is encrypted in transit using TLS 1.2 or higher, and encrypted at rest using AES-256. Nimbus Notes does not currently offer end-to-end encryption, meaning Nimbus Notes staff can technically access note content to provide support, subject to our internal access controls and audit logging.

## Uptime

Nimbus Notes targets 99.9% uptime and publishes real-time status at status.nimbusnotes.example. Business plan customers on an annual contract are eligible for service credits if uptime falls below the 99.9% SLA in a given month.
