# Deleting Your Account or Workspace

## Deleting your personal account

Go to Settings > Account > Delete Account. You'll be asked to confirm by typing your email address. Personal account deletion is immediate and cannot be undone; it does not delete workspaces you don't own.

## Deleting a workspace

Only the workspace owner can delete a workspace, from Settings > Workspace > Danger Zone. Deleting a workspace schedules permanent removal after a 30-day grace period, during which the owner can restore it by contacting support. After 30 days, all notes, attachments, and member data are permanently erased and cannot be recovered.

## Removing a single member

Workspace admins can remove individual members from Settings > Members without deleting the whole workspace. Removed members lose access immediately but their authored notes remain in the workspace.
