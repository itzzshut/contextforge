# Billing and Plans

Nimbus Notes offers three plans: Free, Team, and Business.

The Free plan supports up to 3 team members and 500 notes, with a 10 MB attachment limit per file. The Team plan costs $12 per member per month, billed monthly or annually, and raises the attachment limit to 250 MB. The Business plan costs $24 per member per month and adds single sign-on, audit logs, and a dedicated account manager.

Annual billing gives a 20% discount compared to monthly billing on both the Team and Business plans.

## Changing or cancelling your plan

You can upgrade, downgrade, or cancel your plan at any time from Settings > Billing. Downgrades take effect at the end of the current billing period; upgrades take effect immediately and are prorated for the remaining days in the cycle.

## Refunds

Refunds are issued for annual plans cancelled within 14 days of purchase. Monthly plans are not eligible for refunds, but you will not be charged again after cancelling. To request a refund, email billing@nimbusnotes.example with your workspace ID.

## Payment methods

We accept Visa, Mastercard, and American Express. We do not currently support PayPal, wire transfer, or cryptocurrency payments. Invoices are generated automatically and sent to the billing contact's email on the first of each month.
