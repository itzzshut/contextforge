# Exporting Your Data

You can export your notes at any time; there is no lock-in.

## Export formats

Nimbus Notes supports exporting individual notes or entire workspaces as Markdown, PDF, or HTML. Bulk workspace exports are delivered as a ZIP file containing one file per note plus an `index.json` manifest describing the folder structure and tags.

## Starting an export

Go to Settings > Data > Export Workspace and choose a format. Exports of workspaces under 1,000 notes typically complete within a minute; larger workspaces are processed as a background job and you'll receive an email with a download link when it's ready. Download links expire after 7 days.

## Automated backups

Business plan workspaces can enable nightly automated exports to a connected Amazon S3 or Google Cloud Storage bucket under Settings > Data > Automated Backups.

## What is not included in exports

Exports do not include workspace member activity logs or billing history; those must be requested separately from support.
